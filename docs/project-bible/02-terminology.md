# Chapter 2 — Terminology

**Status:** Stable  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-24

---

> *"A shared language creates a shared vision."*

---

## Purpose

This chapter defines the official vocabulary used throughout The Wayfarer's Ledger.

Consistent terminology creates a consistent experience.

These terms should be used throughout the interface, documentation and source code.

---

# Wayfarer

A Wayfarer is a person who owns and maintains a Ledger.

The word Wayfarer replaces terms such as User, Account or Profile.

A Wayfarer is someone whose journey is worth remembering.

Examples:

- Create a Wayfarer
- Choose a Wayfarer
- Wayfarer Settings

Never:

- User
- User Profile
- Account

---

# Ledger

A Ledger is the personal collection of everything that belongs to one Wayfarer.

It contains:

- Gear
- Outfits
- Festivals
- Journal Entries
- Experience Notes
- Timeline
- Makers
- Wishlist
- Maintenance

Each Wayfarer owns exactly one Ledger.

---

# Vault

The Wayfarer's Vault is the folder that stores all data belonging to one or more Ledgers.

The Vault contains:

- .ledger files
- Photos
- Documents
- Backups
- Exports

The Vault is owned by the Wayfarer.

The application simply accesses it.

---

# Journey

A Journey represents the ongoing history of a Wayfarer.

A Journey begins when a Ledger is created.

It never truly ends.

The Journey is reflected through:

- festivals
- memories
- gear
- timelines
- photographs
- experience notes

---

# Gear

A Gear item represents one physical object.

Examples:

- Boots
- Tunic
- Gorget
- Belt
- Cloak
- Tent
- Drinking Horn

Every physical object is stored separately.

If two identical cloaks exist, they are two Gear items.

---

# Outfit

An Outfit is a reusable combination of Gear.

Outfits are recipes.

They reference Gear.

They do not duplicate Gear.

Outfits evolve over time through version history.

---

# Festival

A Festival represents a real event attended by one or more Wayfarers.

A Festival acts as a memory hub.

It connects:

- photos
- outfits
- journal entries
- experience notes
- maintenance reminders

---

# Journal Entry

A Journal Entry is a free-form written memory.

Journal Entries preserve moments.

Not statistics.

---

# Experience Notes

Experience Notes are practical observations gathered over time.

Unlike reviews, Experience Notes are personal.

Examples:

- Needed thicker socks.
- Excellent in heavy rain.
- Buckle rubbed against my shoulder.

Experience Notes are intended to help future decisions.

---

# Maker

A Maker is the person, company or craftsperson responsible for creating a piece of Gear.

Examples:

- William Lennon
- Steel Mastery
- Black Oak Workshop
- Self-made
- Unknown

A Maker can be connected to many Gear items.

---

# Capture

Capture is the act of creating something quickly.

The goal of Capture is speed.

Completion comes later.

Every Capture should take less than 30 seconds.

---

# Timeline

The Timeline is the chronological history of a Wayfarer's Journey.

It records meaningful moments.

Examples:

- First Festival
- New Boots
- Outfit Version 3
- First Maintenance
- Castlefest 2028

The Timeline is intentionally selective.

It should tell a story rather than record every database change.

---

# Wishlist

The Wishlist stores ideas for future Gear.

Wishlist items may later become Gear.

The Wishlist is intended for remembering possibilities, not managing priorities.

Items may be marked as Favourite.

---

# Maintenance

Maintenance records care performed on Gear.

Examples:

- Oiled leather
- Sharpened blade
- Replaced sole
- Repaired stitching

Maintenance helps preserve both Gear and memories.

---

# AI Assistant

The AI Assistant is an optional helper.

It exists to reduce manual work.

The AI Assistant may:

- identify Gear
- suggest categories
- recognise Makers
- estimate materials
- suggest colours
- propose tags

The AI Assistant must never analyse personal festival memories without explicit permission.

AI assists.

It never replaces the Wayfarer.

---

# First Entry

The first interaction a Wayfarer has with the application.

It marks the beginning of a Journey.

Every Journey begins with a single entry.

---

# Keeper

Keeper is an internal development codename.

The Keeper exists only within the project documentation and development logs.

The Keeper is never visible inside the application.

---

# Language Guidelines

The language of The Wayfarer's Ledger should always be:

- calm
- welcoming
- respectful
- timeless

Avoid:

- corporate language
- gaming terminology
- productivity jargon
- unnecessary technical terms

The application should feel like a trusted travelling companion.

Not a management tool.
